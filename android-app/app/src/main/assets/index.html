3. How to Submit Your Official Appeal

Follow these 4 clear steps to present your appeal directly to WhatsApp:

1

Open WhatsApp & Look for "Request a Review"

Open the WhatsApp app on the banned phone. On the ban notification screen, tap the green "Request a Review" button if it appears.

2

Paste the English Appeal Message

Use the "Copy Message" button above, then paste the text into WhatsApp's review box. (English is strongly recommended because WhatsApp's automated and international support agents review it much faster.)

3

Alternatively: Send an Email to Official Support

If there is no in-app review button or you need to reach them directly, click the blue "Email WhatsApp" button above. It opens a pre-composed email to support@whatsapp.com with the subject and body ready.

4

Wait Patiently (24 to 48 Hours)

Do not spam or send dozens of duplicate requests. WhatsApp reviews accounts individually and typically emails back or unblocks the account within 24 to 48 hours if no severe violation occurred.

4. How to Avoid a WhatsApp Ban in the Future

Keep these essential rules in mind to protect your WhatsApp account from being suspended again:

🚫
Never Use Unofficial / Modded Apps

Delete GBWhatsApp, FMWhatsApp, or WhatsApp Plus immediately. Only use official WhatsApp downloaded from Google Play Store or Apple App Store.

👥
Don't Add Unknown Numbers to Groups

Only add people to WhatsApp groups who already have your number saved in their phone book. Unsolicited group adds lead to instant user reports.

📨
Avoid Bulk Messaging & Forwarding

Do not broadcast automated or duplicate messages to dozens of people who don't know you. WhatsApp's anti-spam algorithms detect this quickly.

💼
Switch to Official WhatsApp Business

If you use WhatsApp for your shop, products, or customer inquiries, download the free official WhatsApp Business app to stay compliant.

⏳
Warm Up New SIM Cards Slowly

If your SIM card or WhatsApp account is new, chat naturally with a few friends first before joining large groups or messaging new contacts.

🤝
Respect Contacts When Blocked

If someone asks you not to message them or blocks you, do not try to contact them from another group. User reports trigger automated bans.
